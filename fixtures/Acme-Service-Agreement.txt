ACME SERVICE AGREEMENT

This synthetic agreement is between Acme Limited (Customer) and Harbor Services Limited (Supplier).

1. Services
The Supplier will provide monthly maintenance services and retain the service records for six years.

2. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving 90 calendar days written notice.

3. Liability
The aggregate liability of the Supplier shall not exceed USD 100,000 in any contract year.

4. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Hong Kong.

5. Renewal
This Agreement will automatically renew for successive one-year terms.

Either party may prevent the extension by giving at least 60 calendar days written notice before the current term expires.

6. Audit
The Customer has the right to audit the Supplier's service records on ten business days written notice.

7. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

8. Insurance
The Supplier shall maintain commercial liability insurance with a limit of USD 1,000,000.

9. Delay
The Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of USD 500 per day of delay, capped at USD 10,000.

10. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
